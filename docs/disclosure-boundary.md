# Disclosure Boundary

The public case study intentionally excludes:

- client identity and usernames;
- private engagement correspondence;
- payment or contract information;
- account numbers, broker credentials, VPS identifiers, and MetaQuotes IDs;
- production MQL4 source code;
- client delivery binaries and private configuration artifacts;
- raw internal screenshots and logs that could expose private context.

Only sanitized architecture, engineering decisions, validation methodology, aggregate test results, and project-status information are published.
